X10 Think / Privacy
Website privacy

Clear by default.

X10 Think does not include advertising trackers in this package. Contact-brief drafts stay in your browser, while download-access requests are stored on the X10 server for manual review.

Contact brief drafts

When you type into the project brief form, the current draft may be saved using browser local storage so it is not lost on refresh. The data stays in that browser unless the site is later connected to a backend or third-party form service.

Download access requests

When you request access to an APK, EXE or other controlled build, X10 stores your name, email address, selected profile category, requested build, stated purpose, request status and download activity. This information is used to review access, deliver approved files and maintain basic release security.

Access tokens and retention

Approved requests receive a unique access token that is tied to the selected build and may expire or be disabled. X10 should periodically remove access-request records that are no longer needed and should not reuse access data for unrelated marketing without a separate lawful basis or consent.

External services

The site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts and may link to external publishing channels. Those services can apply their own privacy policies when you access them.

Future production integrations

The download system may use the hosting provider’s PHP mail service to send request and approval notices. If analytics, CRM or other data-processing services are added later, this notice should be updated before those integrations go live.
